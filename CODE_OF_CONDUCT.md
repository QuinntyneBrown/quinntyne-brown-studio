# Code of Conduct

## Our community

Quinntyne Brown Studio welcomes contributors of every background, identity, experience level, and ability. Everyone should be able to ask questions, learn, and contribute without harassment or discrimination.

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, project discussions, and other spaces where someone is acting as a representative of the project.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with courtesy and respect, including when you disagree.
- Discuss the work and explain your reasoning. Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections.
- Help newcomers understand the project and make room for different experience levels and perspectives.
- Respect privacy, consent, and accessibility needs.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize where appropriate, and adjust your behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, slurs, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing or threatening to publish private information without consent.
- Repeated disruption, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted contact after a request to stop.
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern or participating in a review of conduct.

Technical disagreement and good-faith criticism are welcome when expressed respectfully.

## Reporting concerns

Contact Quinntyne Brown privately at [quinntynebrown@gmail.com](mailto:quinntynebrown@gmail.com), with the subject `Quinntyne Brown Studio conduct report`. Include what happened, relevant links or redacted evidence, and how you prefer to be contacted. Share only information you are comfortable providing; avoid posting sensitive details publicly.

Reports are handled as privately as practical, with information shared only as needed to investigate and respond. The maintainer may ask for context from the people involved and will explain the outcome to the reporter where possible. If a concern involves the maintainer or you are uncomfortable using that channel, you can use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, ask someone to change their behavior, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or permanently exclude a participant. The response considers the severity, impact, and recurrence of the behavior; serious threats or harassment can warrant immediate restrictions.

Participants may request reconsideration by replying privately with relevant context or new information. Maintainers are accountable to the same standards and should step aside from decisions in which they have a personal conflict when another maintainer is available. Response timing depends on maintainer availability.
