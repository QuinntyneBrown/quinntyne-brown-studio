Notifications

Toasts, banners and alerts: priority, grouping and dismissal rules.

WCAG 2.2 AAPattern
  • Overview
  • When to use
  • Structure
  • States
  • Responsive behaviour
  • Accessibility
  • Content
  • Do and don't
  • Sources

Overview

Notifications tell people something happened outside their current focus. Prefer feedback next to the thing that changed; use a notification only when that is not possible.

When to use

TypeUse forDismissal
ToastResult of an action: saved, sent, deleted (with Undo)Auto after 8s or more; pauses on hover/focus; errors persist
AlertState of a page or section that needs attentionStays until resolved or dismissed
Banner (page-level alert)Site-wide notices: coming soon, maintenancePersistent

Structure

A toast with an action, rendered inline.

Print request sent.
Fixed bottom-right in the product; static here.

States

  • One toast at a time per area; new ones replace or queue, never overlap.
  • Stack at most three; older ones leave first.
  • Priority: errors over warnings over success. Errors never auto-dismiss.

Responsive behaviour

  • Desktop: bottom-right, 26rem maximum.
  • Phones: full width above the bottom edge, clear of primary actions.

Accessibility

  • Success and info use role="status" (polite); errors role="alert" (assertive) (4.1.3).
  • Toasts do not take focus; actions inside remain reachable in DOM order after the trigger.
  • Timers pause on hover and keyboard focus and last 8s or more (2.2.1).
  • Never cover the focused element (2.4.11).

Content

  • Past tense and specific: "3 photos deleted."
  • Action verbs match the cause: Undo, View, Retry.
  • No duplicate of a message already visible on the page.
