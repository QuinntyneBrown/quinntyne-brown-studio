Accordion

An accordion stacks short headings that each reveal a block of content. It keeps long, optional answers out of the way until they are wanted.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <details> / <summary>

Overview

Use an accordion for content people scan and open selectively: frequently asked questions, package inclusions, session preparation notes. Do not hide content that most people need (a price, a date), and do not use it for navigation (use the sidebar) or for switching between peer views (use tabs).

  • Multi-open (default): each item opens and closes on its own; people can compare answers.
  • Single-open: give all items the same name attribute; opening one closes the others. Use it when the items are long and comparison is unlikely.
  • With icon: the plus/minus indicator is the system icon. A leading icon is not part of this component; put meaning in the heading text.

The native <details> element is the default; it works without script and exposes expanded state to assistive technology.

Anatomy

  1. Heading<summary>: the question or topic, set in the serif heading style. It is the only control.
  2. IndicatorA + when closed and a minus when open, drawn by CSS, so it is not read aloud.
  3. PanelThe rest of the <details>: muted text with 20px below.

Variants

Both variants share markup; single-open adds one attribute.

Multi-open

How soon will I get my photographs?

Most galleries are ready within three weeks. Weddings take up to six, because there is more to choose from.

Do you travel for sessions?

Parks, venues, kitchens and the end of your own street. Travel is itemized in your quote.

Can I order prints?

Yes. Choose your favourites in your gallery and send a print request; we will confirm sizes and paper with you.

Any number of items can be open. The first is open to show the shape.
HTML
<div class="accordion">
  <details open>
    <summary>How soon will I get my photographs?</summary>
    <p>Most galleries are ready within three weeks.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Do you travel for sessions?</summary>
    <p>Travel is itemized in your quote.</p>
  </details>
</div>

Sizes

An accordion has one size: the heading is at least 44px tall (<code>--target-comfortable</code>).

States

An item is collapsed or expanded; the summary can also be hovered or focused. Hover and focus on the summary use the native pseudo-classes; focus is forced below.

  • Expanded is conveyed by the indicator (a minus) and by the native open attribute, which assistive technology reports as expanded.
  • Disabled items are not supported; remove an item that cannot apply.
  • Deep link: if a URL fragment targets an item, open it on load and move focus to its summary.

Responsive behaviour

  • The accordion is block-level and fills its container at every width; it is one column always.
  • Heading text wraps; the indicator stays at the right, vertically centred, and never wraps below the text.
  • At 390px the heading font stays at 24px; shorten the heading before reducing the size.
  • In a two-column layout put the accordion in the wider column.

Theming

The accordion draws only hairlines in --color-border-default, headings in --color-fg-default and panel text in --color-fg-muted. All three flip with the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native disclosure: <details>/<summary> exposes a button with expanded or collapsed state. This is the simpler cousin of the WAI-ARIA APG Accordion pattern, which uses a heading containing a button with aria-expanded and aria-controls; use that markup if the headings must be in the document outline.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves from one summary to the next; the panel text is not a tab stop unless it contains controls.
Enter, SpaceToggles the focused item.
↓ / ↑Not provided by native details. APG lists these as optional; add them only if the page uses the scripted pattern.
Home / EndOptional in APG; not provided by native details.

Focus

Focus stays on the summary after toggling. The summary shows the shared ring (2px --color-focus-ring, 2px offset). Content revealed below is next in the tab order, so opening an item never traps focus.

Labelling

The summary text is the accessible name. Keep it self-explanatory out of context, because screen reader users navigate by control. If the summary contains a heading element, the heading level fits the page's outline (the mock's FAQ uses h3 for each question).

Motion

The panel opens and closes instantly. If a product animates the height, use --duration-base and respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Each summary is at least 44px tall and spans the full width, so the whole row is the target.

Content

  • Headings are the question or topic, sentence case: "Do you travel for sessions?".
  • Answers are one to three sentences; link to a page for more.
  • Order by how often it is asked, not alphabetically.
  • Do not hide errors, prices or required steps behind an accordion.
  • Use between 3 and 10 items; fewer should simply be shown.
