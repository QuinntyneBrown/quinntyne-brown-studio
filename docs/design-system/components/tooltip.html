Tooltip

A tooltip names or briefly explains a control when it is hovered or focused. It never holds anything the person has to act on.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAPG Tooltip

Overview

Use a tooltip to name an icon-only button, to show a keyboard shortcut, or to expand an abbreviation. If the text is needed to use the control, put it on screen as a visible label. If the content has a link, a button or more than a sentence, use a popover or a dialog; tooltips cannot contain interactive content.

  • Text: one short phrase, 20rem wide at most.
  • With shortcut: the phrase plus a <kbd> showing the key, for example "Next photo" with →.

The tooltip is additional: the control still has its own accessible name, and touch users, who cannot hover, never depend on it.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerAny focusable control. It has its own name and aria-describedby pointing at the tooltip.
  2. Tooltip.tooltip with role="tooltip": inverse surface, caption text, 8px/12px padding, shadow-3.

Variants

Place the tooltip 8px from its trigger, preferably above, and keep it inside the viewport.

Sizes

Tooltips have one size: caption text (12px) in a box up to 20rem wide that wraps.

States

A tooltip is hidden or visible. It becomes visible on hover or keyboard focus of the trigger, and hides when the pointer leaves, focus moves away, or Esc is pressed. The matrix forces the trigger states and shows the tooltip as it appears.

  • Hidden (default): the tooltip element is not rendered, or has hidden. The trigger shows only its own look.
  • Delay: appears 300ms after pointer hover so moving across a toolbar does not flash tooltips; appears immediately on keyboard focus; disappears immediately.
  • Persistent: stays while the pointer moves from the trigger onto the tooltip (WCAG 1.4.13 hoverable), and until focus or pointer leaves, or Esc is pressed (dismissible).
  • Disabled triggers do not show tooltips; explain unavailability in nearby text.

Responsive behaviour

  • Position above the trigger by default; flip below when there is no room. Shift sideways to stay at least 8px inside the viewport.
  • On touch screens there is no hover. Do not rely on tooltips; if the same help is needed, show it as visible text or in a popover opened by tap.
  • Width is capped at 20rem and text wraps; keep the text short enough to be one or two lines at 390px.
  • At 200-400% zoom the tooltip scales with the text and must not be clipped by an overflow-hidden ancestor; render it in the top layer.

Theming

The tooltip is the inverse surface (--color-bg-inverse with --color-fg-inverse): near-black on white in light, near-white on dark in dark. The shortcut key uses --color-border-inverse for its border. Shadow is --shadow-3.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA APG Tooltip: the tooltip has role="tooltip" and the trigger references it with aria-describedby. It describes; it does not name. The trigger keeps its own accessible name (aria-label or visible text).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabFocusing the trigger shows the tooltip; moving focus away hides it. The tooltip itself is never a tab stop.
EscHides the tooltip without moving focus (WCAG 1.4.13 dismissible).
Enter, SpaceActivate the trigger as normal; the tooltip does not change that.

Focus

Focus stays on the trigger. The tooltip never receives focus, so it cannot hold links or buttons. The trigger shows the shared ring (2px --color-focus-ring, 2px offset) and the tooltip must not cover it.

Labelling

Give the tooltip a unique id and reference it with aria-describedby. Do not put the control's name only in the tooltip: an icon-only button needs aria-label. The shortcut key's border is decorative; the key glyph carries the meaning. Do not use the title attribute, which is unreliable for keyboard and touch users.

Motion

The tooltip appears and disappears without animation. If a product fades it in, use --duration-fast and drop the fade under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Triggers are 44px buttons. The tooltip is not touch-reachable, so it must never be the only place a fact or name appears.

Content

  • One short phrase, sentence case, no full stop: "Download selection".
  • Name the action, not the icon: "Download selection", not "Download icon".
  • Shortcut tooltips read "Next photograph" followed by the key.
  • Never put links, buttons or form fields inside.
  • Do not repeat a visible label.
