Cost summary

The cost summary lists what a quote or print order costs, line by line, and ends with one total. It updates live as people change their choices.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableLive region

Overview

Use a cost summary beside the quote calculator, on the quote summary page and at the end of a print request. Use a description list for non-monetary facts and a table when a bill has many columns (quantity, unit price, tax).

  • Rows are a label and an amount.
  • Discount rows are tinted with the success tone and show a minus sign.
  • The total row sits below a rule with the amount in the serif display style.
  • Amounts are Canadian dollars unless the row says otherwise, with a currency note on the total.

Anatomy

  1. Row labelWhat is charged, in sentence case with the quantity: "Wedding coverage, 6 hours".
  2. AmountRight-aligned figure with the currency symbol and cents.
  3. Discount rowSame layout, success ink, minus sign. Text says what the discount is.
  4. Total rowA hairline above, body-lg label, serif 2xl amount.

Variants

The structure is always rows then a total. Only the rows change.

Recalculating keeps the label and replaces the figure with a skeleton; the error never shows a stale total as final.

Sizes

The summary has one size; it fills its container (a sidebar column or a card).

States

A summary has data states instead of interaction states. Any actions (Request quote) belong in a button below, not in the rows.

  • Updating: change the numbers in place; avoid fading or counting up. Announce the new total once.
  • Rounding: show cents on every row and the total, even when whole.
  • Taxes: add a row "HST (13%)" before the total, or say "before taxes" in the total label; never leave it ambiguous.

Responsive behaviour

  • Rows are flex; label wraps, amount stays on the first line to the right. They hold down to 320px.
  • In the two-column quote layout the summary is sticky in the right column from 1100px; below that it sits under the form, above the submit button.
  • The total amount never wraps; very long labels wrap instead.

Theming

Rows use default ink, discounts --color-success-fg, the total rule --color-border-default. The total figure uses --font-family-serif at --font-size-2xl. No overrides are needed for dark mode.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A labelled role="group" of text rows; the total is a polite live region (role="status") so the new amount is announced after a change, following the live-region guidance. Not a table: rows are label and value pairs. If the bill needs columns, use a table.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabNo stop: the summary is static. A Retry button in the error state is reachable.
Enter, SpaceActivates Retry.
EscNo effect.

Focus

No focus target in the normal state. When the calculation fails, do not steal focus; the alert is announced and Retry stays in the tab order.

Labelling

The group is named "Quote summary". Discounts must be negative in text ("−$200.00") and in the label ("discount"). The live region wraps only the total so assistive technology is not flooded.

Contrast

Discount row on surface

Motion

Amounts change instantly; the skeleton shimmer stops under reduced motion.

Touch

No targets in the summary itself. The submit and Retry buttons are the standard 44px and 32px buttons.

Content

  • Amounts have the currency symbol and two decimals: "$2,400.00". Negative amounts use the true minus sign (−), not a hyphen.
  • Say "Estimated total" while the quote can change, "Total" once confirmed.
  • State the currency once, on the total: "(CAD)".
  • Discounts name their reason: "Engagement session discount", not "Discount".
  • Never present an estimate as a price you will charge unless the studio has confirmed it.
