Menu

A menu gathers a short list of actions behind one trigger, so a row or header can offer several choices without showing them all.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAPG Menu Button

Overview

Use a menu for actions on an object: "Edit", "Duplicate", "Delete" on a session row, or an account menu in the admin header. Do not use it for navigation (use top bar or sidebar), for picking a value in a form (use select), or for one or two actions (show buttons).

  • Standard items run an action and close the menu.
  • With icons and shortcuts help scanning in dense admin screens.
  • Sections are split by a separator; destructive items always sit last.
  • Checkable items (role="menuitemcheckbox") toggle a setting such as "Show captions".

The menu panel is documented here as a static block. In the product it is positioned beneath its trigger and layered at --z-dropdown.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerA button with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.
  2. Panelrole="menu" on a raised surface, --shadow-3, --radius-md.
  3. ItemA button with role="menuitem", 32px minimum height; optional icon and shortcut hint.
  4. SeparatorA hairline between groups; the destructive group is last.

Variants

Menus vary in what an item can carry. Keep one menu to one kind of item where possible.

With icons, shortcut hints and a danger item

The destructive item is red, separated, and last.
HTML
<div class="menu" role="menu" aria-label="Session actions">
  <button class="menu__item" type="button" role="menuitem">Edit session</button>
  <button class="menu__item" type="button" role="menuitem">Duplicate session</button>
  <hr class="menu__sep">
  <button class="menu__item menu__item--danger" type="button" role="menuitem">Delete session</button>
</div>

Checkable items

A tick appears when aria-checked="true". The menu stays open until dismissed when the setting has a live preview.

Account menu

Navigation-like items are allowed in the account menu only because it is an account control.

Sizes

The menu has one size. Item height is the 32px small control height and the panel is at least 12rem wide; it widens to its longest label.

States

Items have four states. Hover and selected (the keyboard-active item) share one background; forced here with data-state and aria-selected.

Item
Danger item

Both themes

  • Open and closed belong to the trigger: aria-expanded="true|false". A closed menu is removed from the accessibility tree.
  • Disabled items use aria-disabled="true" so they stay in the arrow-key order and screen readers can announce them.
  • Empty menu is never shown; hide the trigger when there are no actions.

Responsive behaviour

  • The panel opens below the trigger and flips above when there is no room. It is at most the viewport width minus --space-4 on each side.
  • At 390px an admin row menu may open as a bottom sheet with the same items, each 44px tall; the roles and keys do not change.
  • Labels never wrap. A label that does not fit at 390px is too long.

Theming

The panel uses --color-bg-surface-raised with --shadow-3; in the dark theme elevation is carried by the lighter surface and a border (--color-border-default) rather than by the shadow alone. Items read --color-fg-default; danger items read --color-danger-fg. Both pass 4.5:1 in either theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA APG Menu Button. The trigger is a button with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded; the panel is role="menu"; items are menuitem, menuitemcheckbox or menuitemradio. Menus are for actions, not for site navigation.

Keyboard

KeyAction

↑On the trigger: opens and focuses the last item. In the menu: previous item, wrapping.

Focus

Opening moves focus into the menu onto the first enabled item (roving focus: only the focused item is a tab stop). Closing returns focus to the trigger. The focused item shows the shared ring and the item background.

Labelling

The panel has an aria-label (or aria-labelledby pointing at the trigger) such as "Session actions". Items are named by their visible text; icons are aria-hidden. Shortcut hints are part of the name and read after the label.

Motion

The panel appears over --duration-fast with a short fade; under prefers-reduced-motion it appears instantly.

Touch

Items are 32px tall on pointer devices, which meets the 24px minimum; on coarse pointers they grow to 44px with 8px separation.

Content

  • Items are verb phrases: "Edit session", "Duplicate session", "Delete session". Sentence case.
  • Order by frequency, put destructive items last behind a separator.
  • Keep the menu to seven items or fewer. More than that needs a different pattern.
  • Name the object when several menus share a screen ("Edit session", not "Edit").
