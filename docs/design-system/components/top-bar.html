Component &middot; Navigation

Top bar

The top bar names the product and gives the way to every top-level destination. It comes in two forms: the public site header and the admin app bar.

Overview

Every page has exactly one top bar. It stays the same from page to page so people always know where they are and where they can go (WCAG 3.2.3). The destinations inside it are plain links; the one call to action is a button-styled link.

  • Site header (.site-header) is used on the marketing and client sites: brand on the left, .site-nav on the right, a "Find your quote" call to action as the last item.
  • App bar (.app-bar) is used inside the admin workspace next to the sidebar: it shows where you are (workspace / page) and who you are signed in as. It carries no destinations; the sidebar does.
  • Compact (mobile): below 768px the nav collapses behind a .nav-toggle button that opens a full-width panel.

Skip past it with a skip link. Tabs that switch content inside a page belong in tabs, not in the top bar.

Anatomy

  1. Brand.brand: uppercase wordmark with the descriptor underneath. Always a link to the home page.
  2. Nav links.site-nav a; the current page carries aria-current="page" and a 1px underline.
  3. Call to actionAt most one, last in the nav, styled as a small button. It is a link, so it keeps href.

Use the site header for public and client pages; use the app bar for the admin workspace.

Sizes

The top bar has one size per form: the site header is at least <code>--layout-header-height</code> (104px) and the app bar at least <code>--layout-topbar-height</code> (72px).

States

Top-bar states live on the links and the menu button inside it. The bar itself has no states. Hover on nav links changes the colour to --color-fg-link-hover through :hover only, so it cannot be forced for review; focus and current are shown below.

  • Menu open: on narrow screens aria-expanded="true" on the toggle and .is-open (or data-state="open") on .site-nav show the panel, which sits under the bar and spans its full width.
  • Current page is conveyed by the underline and aria-current="page"; never by colour alone.

Responsive behaviour

  • At 768px and wider the nav is inline. Below 768px (--layout-breakpoint-md) .nav-toggle appears and .site-nav becomes a full-width panel that drops from the bottom edge of the bar.
  • In the open panel each link is at least 44px tall (--target-comfortable) and the call to action stays last.
  • Horizontal padding follows --layout-margin: 16px on phones, 32px at 768px, 48px at 1100px.
  • The brand never wraps or truncates; the descriptor line below it is part of the wordmark.
  • Seven links fit at 1100px. Past about seven top-level destinations, group the extras under one link rather than shrinking the type.

Theming

The bar reads --color-bg-canvas and --color-border-default; links use --color-fg-default and hover to --color-fg-link-hover. Both flip with the theme; no component token is needed. The mobile panel uses --color-bg-surface-raised so it separates from the page in the dark theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <header> landmark containing a <nav> with its own aria-label. This is the WAI-ARIA APG Navigation landmark pattern; the collapsed menu uses the Disclosure navigation pattern, not role="menu".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through brand, toggle (mobile), links and the call to action in DOM order.
EnterFollows the focused link.
Enter, SpaceOn the menu toggle: opens or closes the panel.
EscCloses an open mobile panel and returns focus to the toggle.

Focus

Links and the toggle use the shared focus ring (2px --color-focus-ring, 2px offset). The bar is not sticky by default; if a product makes it sticky, set scroll-padding-top to the bar height so focused content is never hidden behind it (WCAG 2.4.11).

Labelling

Name the landmark when a page has more than one nav: "Main" for the public site, "Client" for the client site, "Footer" for the footer. The toggle's visible text "Menu" is its name; aria-expanded and aria-controls state and target. The avatar is decorative and hidden because the name is printed beside it.

Motion

The bar has no motion of its own. The mobile panel appears without animation, so there is nothing to reduce; if a product animates it, use --duration-base and honour prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Nav links are 44px tall in the mobile panel. On desktop the links have 8px of vertical padding and 24px gaps, which meets the 24px minimum target with spacing.

Content

  • Nav labels are one or two words, nouns, sentence case: "Portfolio", "Print requests". No verbs except the call to action.
  • The call to action is an action: "Find your quote", not "Quote".
  • Keep labels identical across pages and between the header and footer (WCAG 3.2.4).
  • The descriptor under the brand is a short noun phrase ("Photography studio"). Do not put a tagline there.
  • Show the signed-in person as "Name · Sign out" so the button explains itself.
