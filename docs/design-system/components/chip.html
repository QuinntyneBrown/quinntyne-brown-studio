Chip

A chip is a compact token for a filter, a selection or a tag. Filter chips toggle, removable chips take themselves out, static chips just label.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableToggle button

Overview

Use a chip for choices people can switch on and off ("Weddings", "Headshots") or for items they have added ("Toronto"). Use a badge for a read-only status, tabs to switch views, and a checkbox group when the choices belong in a form.

  • Filter chip is a button with aria-pressed; it narrows a list in place.
  • Removable chip carries a labelled remove button.
  • Static chip labels without interaction: a tag on a blog post.

Anatomy

  1. LabelOne or two words in caption type. It is the accessible name.
  2. Remove button (optional)A ghost icon button with a specific label. Only on removable chips.

Variants

Pick by what the chip does. All variants share the same 32px height and square-ish corners.

Filter chips

The pressed chip is filled with --color-brand-subtle and outlined in --color-brand.
HTML
<div class="cluster" role="group" aria-label="Filter sessions by type">
  <button class="chip" type="button" aria-pressed="true">All</button>
  <button class="chip" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Weddings</button>
</div>

Static chips are text. They are not focusable and do not look interactive; if they hover, make them links.

Sizes

A chip has one size (32px, <code>--control-height-sm</code>); a larger target is a button or a card.

States

Filter chips share the button's states. Pointer and focus are forced with data-state here; real pages use :hover and :focus-visible.

Both themes

Outdoor
Outdoor
  • Pressed uses aria-pressed="true". Do not change the label when pressed; the state is exposed separately.
  • Disabled uses the native attribute and is not focusable. Prefer leaving a chip enabled and explaining why it finds no results.
  • Result of a filter: announce the new count in a live region such as the list heading ("12 sessions"); see data tables and lists.

Responsive behaviour

  • Chips wrap onto new rows inside a .cluster; they never scroll sideways or truncate their label.
  • A very long chip group (more than about eight) moves into a select or a menu below 768px.
  • Chip height is 32px; on touch screens leave 8px between chips and keep the group at least 44px from other targets, or raise the chip to a tab.

Theming

Chips read the surface, border and brand-subtle tokens. Pressed uses --color-brand-subtle and --color-brand; both swap in the dark theme. Override --color-brand on a wrapper to re-colour the pressed outline for another product.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A filter chip is a toggle button: native <button type="button"> with aria-pressed, see the APG button pattern. A group of chips is a role="group" with an aria-label. Removable chips contain a real button.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves between chips and remove buttons.
Enter, SpaceToggles a filter chip or removes a removable one.
Backspace, DeleteOn a focused remove button, removes the chip (optional shortcut).
EscNo effect.

Focus

The shared 2px ring appears on the chip or the remove button on :focus-visible. After removal, focus moves to the next chip, or to the previous one, or to the input that owns the chips if none remain.

Labelling

The text is the name; aria-pressed is the state. Remove buttons use "Remove <label>". Announce removals and filter results in a live region.

Motion

No animation on toggle. Background changes instantly.

Touch

The chip is 32px tall and meets the 24px minimum. Keep 8px between chips. Where chips are the primary control on a phone, raise them with the large control height or use tabs.

Content

  • One or two words in sentence case: "Family portraits".
  • Chips for filters name the attribute value, not the action: "Weddings", not "Filter by weddings".
  • Do not mix filter and static chips in one row.
  • Do not add an icon unless it removes ambiguity.
