Typography

Georgia headings, Arial body, nine sizes and twelve role shorthands.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Two families: Georgia (serif) for headings, prices and numerals, Arial (sans) for everything else. Headings are regular weight with tight tracking, which gives the editorial feel of the mocks; small uppercase eyebrows with wide tracking label sections and table columns.

Families

Serif — Georgia, Times New Roman

Sans — Arial, Helvetica

Mono — ui-monospace, Menlo, Consolas

System font stacks only: no web-font download, no layout shift.

Size scale

Nine sizes. Nothing renders below 12px: the mocks' 9-11px eyebrows and badges are snapped up to --font-size-xs.

Role shorthands

Components use a role, never a size plus weight plus line height. Each role is a font shorthand token.

--text-display
Photography for the days that matter
--text-h2
Choose your session
--text-h4
Delivery details
--text-body
Pick a date, a location and how many hours you need; the quote updates as you go.
--text-caption
Updated 12 minutes ago
--text-overline
Upcoming sessions

Weights, leading and tracking

  • Weights: regular 400 (headings, body), medium 500 (buttons), semibold 600 (labels, eyebrows), bold 700 (brand wordmark only).
  • Line height: 1.1 headings, 1.35 snug, 1.6 body, 1.75 long-form article text.
  • Tracking: --letter-spacing-tight on serif headings, --letter-spacing-caps (0.14em) on uppercase eyebrows and table heads.

Measure and long-form

Prose is capped at --layout-measure (65ch). Article bodies use 1.75 leading and a lead paragraph in --text-body-lg. Never centre long paragraphs.

Usage

  • One <h1> per page in --text-h1 (or --text-display on the marketing home hero only).
  • Prices, stat numbers and the cost total use the serif with tabular numerals.
  • Sentence case everywhere; uppercase only through the eyebrow role, never typed in capitals.
  • The display size is fluid (clamp, 40-72px). Other sizes are fixed in rem and scale with the browser's text size.

Accessibility

  • All sizes are rem, so user font-size settings and 200% zoom reflow without clipping (1.4.4, 1.4.10).
  • Content survives text-spacing overrides (line height 1.5, letter spacing 0.12em, word spacing 0.16em) because no container has a fixed height around text (1.4.12).
  • Large text (24px+ or 18.7px bold) has a 3:1 minimum; the system keeps 4.5:1 everywhere regardless.
