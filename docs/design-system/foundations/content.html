Content

Voice, casing, numbers and the message formulas for errors, empty states and confirmations.

Overview

The studio speaks like a thoughtful photographer: warm, plain, specific. Copy is short and says what will happen. The full guidance for forms and notifications is in content and tone.

Voice and tone

  • Warm and direct: "Your gallery is ready", not "Gallery provisioning complete".
  • Name things the way clients do: session, gallery, album, print, quote.
  • Reassure on errors, never blame: "We couldn't send your request. Nothing was lost; try again."

Casing and punctuation

  • Sentence case for headings, buttons, labels and navigation. Proper nouns keep their capitals.
  • Uppercase appears only through the eyebrow style.
  • No exclamation marks except genuine celebration; no ampersands in running text.

Numbers, money, dates

  • Currency is CAD: CAD 96.00 or $96.00 with the currency stated once per page. Quotes are estimates, and the page says so.
  • Dates: Sat, Jun 6, 2026; times in the studio's time zone with the zone named on first use, e.g. 2:30 pm EDT.
  • Durations: 6 hours; distances in kilometres.

Message formulas

MessageFormulaExample
Field errorWhat is wrong + how to fixEnter a date at least two days from now.
Empty stateTitle + one sentence + one actionNo sessions yet. Create your first session to start scheduling. [Create session]
Success toastPast-tense result, optional UndoSession saved. [Undo]
FailureWhat failed + what is safe + the next stepThe upload stopped at 4 of 6 photos. Finished photos are saved. [Retry 2 photos]
ConfirmationVerb + object, consequence in bodyDelete 3 photos? They will be removed from the gallery and cannot be restored.

Usage

  • Write the real string, never lorem ipsum, in mocks and examples.
  • Keep button labels to three words; a label that wraps at 390px is too long.
  • Link text makes sense alone: "View session QB-0412", not "click here" (2.4.4).

Accessibility

  • Plain language; avoid idioms and abbreviations without expansion.
  • Error and status messages are programmatically associated with their source (3.3.1, 4.1.3).
  • Set lang on foreign phrases (3.1.2).
