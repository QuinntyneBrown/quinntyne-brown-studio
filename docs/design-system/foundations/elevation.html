Elevation

Four shadow levels, surface hierarchy and a nine-layer z-order.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • Shadow levels
  • Surface hierarchy
  • Z-order
  • Usage
  • Accessibility
  • Tokens

Overview

The mocks are flat: borders, not shadows, separate content. Elevation is reserved for things that float: hovered cards, menus, toasts and dialogs. Four levels were harvested and are kept.

Shadow levels

--shadow-2
--shadow-3
--shadow-4
--shadow-focus
  • 1 floating labels and prototype chrome, 2 hovered cards, 3 menus and toasts, 4 dialogs and the lightbox.
  • --shadow-focus is a two-ring alternative to outline for the rare control that cannot use outline-offset.

Surface hierarchy

  • Light: canvas and surface are both white; hierarchy comes from borders and the sunken/subtle tints.
  • Dark: canvas < surface < raised get lighter as they rise, and shadows deepen because they are barely visible on dark surfaces.
  • Backdrop (--color-bg-backdrop) dims content behind modal dialogs.

Z-order

TokenValue
--z-base
--z-sticky
--z-overlay
--z-popover
--z-tooltip

A component never sets a raw z-index. Mocks used 20, 25, 30, 90, 100; they map to sticky, dropdown, toast and skip.

Usage

  • Do not add a shadow to a bordered card at rest.
  • Never stack more than two floating layers (menu over dialog is the maximum).
  • Toasts sit above dialogs so confirmations stay visible.

Accessibility

  • Elevation is never the only cue for state; selected and focused items also change border or outline.
  • Fixed layers (toasts, sticky headers) must not hide the focused element: use scroll-padding-top and keep toasts clear of controls (2.4.11).
